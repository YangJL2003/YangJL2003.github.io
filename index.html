Junlin Yang

I'm a first-year PhD student in Tsinghua C3I Lab, advised by Ning Ding and Bowen Zhou.

Previously, I worked as a research intern at The University of Hong Kong with Prof. Tao Yu, and at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign with Prof. Hao Peng.

Feel free to reach out about research, collaborations, or just for a friendly chat.

Research vision

I build agents for digital and physical worlds—modeling the tasks they face, scaling how they learn and act, and pushing them toward self-evolution.
